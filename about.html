What Is A Cat Cafe?

A cat cafe is similar to a traditional cafe, with the notable exception of kittens and cats that live there 24/7. The cats are kept healthy, given proper meals, treats, water, and plenty of affection. Customers can book a visit with the cats at any time or stop by just like they would a regular cafe!

Our Mission

It is our goal to provide a cozy and quiet place that lets you, our patrons, pause a while. We have created a home away from home where you can come to have a great cup of coffee or tea, eat a home-baked treat, and meet a furry friend.

How We Make A Difference

At Paws Awhile we do our best to be involved with the community and provide for the cats in our care. To this end, we have partnered with various maritime shelters to help cats without a home to find their forever home. We aim to provide an interactive experience for both potential adopters and animal lovers alike.
