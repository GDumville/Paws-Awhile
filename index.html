PEI's Own Cat Cafe!

Welcome to Paws Awhile, PEIs first cat cafe! It is our goal to provide a cozy home away from home for both people and felines from various atlantic animal shelters can pause awhile, socialize, and have a treat. Patrons are able to stop by any time to purchase some of our home-baked goods, sit down and have a coffee or tea, or book a visit with the cats!

To find out more about us, about our menu, or about the cats we have at the cafe please check out the relevant links at the top of our site. If you would like to book online hit Book A Visit Now!
